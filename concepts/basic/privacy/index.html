Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. KHIPUAI is a sole proprietorship based in Ontario, Canada, serving clients across North and South America..

Policy Scope

This policy explains what information KHIPUAI collects through this website, how it is used, and your choices. It is written in plain language and kept deliberately short.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide. If you book a call, we collect the details you provide to Calendly (typically your name, email, company, and any message you include). Your self-assessment answers stay in your browser; opening the booking link shares your score, result band, and top areas with Calendly. If you request your result by email, we store your first name, email, company (if given), score, result band, top areas, and whether you agreed to a follow-up in our own Google account, and email you the result once. We follow up only if you checked the consent box.
  • Website activity. We use Google Analytics to understand visits and referring sources. If you allow Meta measurement, Meta Pixel receives page views, whether an assessment score was viewed, and whether a call was booked. We do not send your answers or score to Meta.

How We Use Information

  • To send you the resource you requested (such as the self-assessment).
  • To respond to you, schedule a call, and follow up about Solution Design and our AI automation solutions.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which content and channels are useful.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

Who Processes Your Data

We use reputable third-party tools to run this site and our outreach (for example, a scheduling tool, an email or form provider, and website analytics). Each processes data only to provide its service. If you book a call or opt in, your details are stored in those tools and in our own contact records.

Confidentiality of Client Work

Any business information a client shares during an engagement is treated as confidential and is never published or shared without written permission. Case examples on this site are anonymized.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from follow-ups, at any time. Email alexp@khipuai.co and we will handle your request.

You can revisit your Meta measurement choice at any time.

Data Security

We use least-privilege access and reputable, secured services. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
