Definition

What is Solution Design?

Solution Design answers three questions about your business with evidence instead of opinions: where your team's hours actually go, which of that work a machine could do reliably, and what each fix is worth in saved hours and dollars. Then it turns those answers into the design of a solution you will own, not a software demo.

It is different from hiring a consultancy (open-ended meter, long discovery) and different from a vendor pitch (the answer is always their platform). Solution Design is fixed-fee, fixed-timeline, and tool-neutral: the solution builds into the systems you already run, like your existing ERP, ticketing, and Microsoft 365 stack.

If there is not much worth automating, we tell you so. A candid answer is part of the value.

Timing

When does your team need it?

You do not need a transformation program. Solution Design is worth it when several of these sound familiar:

  • People retype data from PDFs and emails into your systems every week
  • The same report gets rebuilt by hand every week or month
  • Approvals crawl across inboxes and spreadsheets with no owner
  • Headcount in admin roles grows with revenue instead of staying flat
  • Leadership wants to use AI, but no one has identified where it would help

Each of those is a measurable cost. Solution Design counts it before you spend anything fixing it.

Process

From first call to a product you own.

  1. 01

    Discovery call (free, 20 to 30 minutes)

    We confirm there is a fit and pick the areas worth looking at. No preparation needed on your side.

  2. 02

    Walkthrough (about one week)

    A few working sessions with you and your team to see the real workflows: the systems, the handoffs, the manual steps. Read-only; nothing changes in your systems while we design.

  3. 03

    Solution design (week 2)

    You get the design of the solution, the plan and the price to build it, and a live call to walk through it with you and your team.

  4. 04

    Build (optional)

    We build it, or your team or anyone else does. Either way, the product is yours.

Questions

Common questions about Solution Design.

How long does Solution Design take?

About two weeks. Week one is a few working sessions to see the real workflows. Week two you get the design, the plan and the price, and a live call to walk through them. Your team's total time commitment is a couple of hours.

What access do you need from us?

A couple of hours of your team's time across a week, and a look at the workflows and systems involved, usually over screen shares. Access is read-only and nothing changes in your systems while we design. Building comes later, and only if you choose it.

Is Solution Design a sales pitch for a build?

No. The design is yours to act on with anyone, including your own team. If you do build with KHIPUAI within 30 days, the full fee credits toward the build. And if there is not much worth automating, we tell you so plainly.

What kinds of work usually get automated?

The recurring offenders are document intake and data entry, hand-built reports, reconciliations between systems that do not talk, approval chasing across inboxes, and copying the same data into multiple tools. Solution Design puts hours and dollars on each one before recommending anything.

Do you guarantee the savings numbers?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Solution Design uses conservative estimates built from your team's actual volumes and time, so you can judge each opportunity on evidence.

Do you need to be on site?

No. Solution Design runs over video calls and screen shares, on US time zones.
