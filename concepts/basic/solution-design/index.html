Definition

What is Solution Design?

Evidence instead of opinions: where your team's hours go, which of that work a machine could do reliably, and what each fix is worth. Then the design of a solution you will own, built into the systems you already run.

Fixed fee, fixed timeline and tool-neutral. If there is not much worth automating, we tell you so.

Questions

Common questions about Solution Design.

How long does Solution Design take?

About two weeks. Week one is a few working sessions to see the real workflows. In week two you get the design, the plan and the price, and a live call to walk through them. Your team's total time commitment is a couple of hours.

What access do you need from us?

A couple of hours of your team's time across a week, and a look at the workflows and systems involved, usually over screen shares. Access is read-only and nothing changes in your systems while we design. Building comes later, and only if you choose it.

Is Solution Design a sales pitch for a build?

No. The design is yours to act on with anyone, including your own team. If you do build with KHIPUAI within 30 days, the full fee credits toward the build. And if there is not much worth automating, we tell you so plainly.

What kinds of work usually get automated?

The recurring offenders are document intake and data entry, hand-built reports, reconciliations between systems that do not talk, approval chasing across inboxes, and copying the same data into multiple tools. Solution Design puts hours and dollars on each one before recommending anything.

Do you guarantee the savings numbers?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Solution Design uses conservative estimates built from your team's actual volumes and time, so you can judge each opportunity on evidence.

Do you need to be on site?

No. Solution Design runs over video calls and screen shares, on US time zones.
