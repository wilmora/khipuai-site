Security and data governance

Building with AI does not mean handing your records to a third party. This is how we keep sensitive information safe, while we design and in everything we build.

How we use AI

  • Built with test data

    AI writes and tests the code with made-up or redacted data. Your records never go to an AI model while we build.

  • The code does the work

    What we deliver reads, sorts and drafts the same way every time, inside your systems.

  • AI in live systems

    Where a running system uses AI, it goes through Anthropic’s business API, under terms that do not use the data to train AI models. Never consumer chat apps.

  • Sensitive fields kept out

    Details like social security numbers and bank accounts are masked or left out of what the AI sees.

  • People approve

    A person approves anything that moves money, changes a record or commits you to a customer.

  • A complete activity record

    Every action the system prepares is logged with who approved it and when.

Access and storage

  • Your systems, your data

    Wherever possible we build inside your own environment, such as Microsoft Azure, and keep no copies.

  • Least-privilege access

    Separate accounts with only the access the job needs, removed when the work ends.

  • Read-only while we design

    Nothing changes in your systems during Solution Design.

  • Secrets in a vault

    API keys and passwords stay server-side, for example in Azure Key Vault, never in code.

Agreements and the end of an engagement

  • NDA on request

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement or a data processing agreement on request.

  • Returned or deleted

    At the end of an engagement, your data is returned or deleted.

Questions from your IT or security team

Send them to alexp@khipuai.co or raise them on a free discovery call. Please do not send passwords or client records by email; we agree on a secure way to share them first.

We are writing these practices up as policies and procedures your IT team can review; each will be listed here when finished. How this website handles your details is in the privacy policy.
