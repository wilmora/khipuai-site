Security and Data Governance

Building with AI does not require handing your records to a third party. This is how we protect sensitive information during design and in everything we build.

The Approach

  • Built with Test Data

    AI writes and tests the code with synthetic or redacted data. Your records are never sent to an AI model during the build.

  • The Code Does the Work

    Our deliverables read, sort and draft the same way every time, inside your systems.

  • AI in Live Systems

    Where a running system uses AI, it goes through Anthropic’s business API, under terms that do not use the data to train AI models. Consumer chat applications are never used.

  • Sensitive Fields Excluded

    Details such as Social Security numbers and bank accounts are masked or excluded from what the AI sees.

  • Human Approval

    A person approves anything that moves money, changes a record or commits you to a customer.

  • A Complete Activity Record

    Every action the system prepares is logged with who approved it and when.

Access and Storage

  • Your Systems, Your Data

    Wherever possible we build inside your own environment, such as Microsoft Azure, and keep no copies.

  • Least-Privilege Access

    Separate accounts with only the access the work requires, removed when the engagement ends.

  • Read-Only During Design

    Nothing changes in your systems during Solution Design.

  • Secrets in a Vault

    API keys and passwords stay server-side, for example in Azure Key Vault, never in code.

Agreements and Engagement Close

  • NDA on Request

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement or a data processing agreement on request.

  • Returned or Deleted

    At the end of an engagement, your data is returned or deleted.

Questions from Your IT or Security Team

Please send them to alexp@khipuai.co or raise them on a free discovery call. Please do not send passwords or client records by email; we will first agree on a secure way to share them.
