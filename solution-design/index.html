Definition

What is Solution Design?

Evidence instead of opinions: where your team's hours go, which of that work a machine could do reliably, and what each fix is worth. Then the design of a solution you will own, built into the systems you already run.

Fixed fee, fixed timeline and tool-neutral. If there is little worth automating, we will say so.

FAQs

Common questions about Solution Design.

How long does Solution Design take?

About two weeks. Week one consists of a few working sessions to review the actual workflows. In week two you receive the design, the plan and the price, along with a live call to review them. Your team's total time commitment is a couple of hours.

What access do you need from us?

A couple of hours of your team's time over one week, and visibility into the workflows and systems involved. Access is read-only and nothing changes in your systems during design. Building comes later, and only if you choose to proceed.

Is Solution Design a sales pitch for a build?

No. The design is yours to act on with anyone, including your own team. If you build with KHIPUAI within 30 days, the full fee credits toward the build. If there is little worth automating, we will say so clearly.

What kinds of work usually get automated?

The most common candidates are document intake and data entry, manually built reports, reconciliations between disconnected systems, approval follow-up across inboxes, and copying the same data into multiple tools. Solution Design quantifies each one in hours and dollars before recommending anything.

Do you guarantee the savings numbers?

No, and any guarantee of that kind deserves caution. Solution Design uses conservative estimates built from your team's actual volumes and time, so you can judge each opportunity on evidence.

Do you need to be on site?

No. Solution Design runs virtually.
